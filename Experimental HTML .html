EcoTrack AI

An intelligent dashboard that helps campus students track and reduce their carbon footprint in real-time.

Key Features

⚡ Real-Time Tracking

Monitors daily food waste and electricity consumption through simple user uploads.

🤖 AI Suggestions

Generates tailored tips every morning to lower energy usage based on historical habits.

🏆 Campus Leaderboard

Gamifies sustainability by allowing hostel blocks and batches to compete for rewards.
